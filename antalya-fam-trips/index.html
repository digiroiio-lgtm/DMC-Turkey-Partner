Antalya Fam Trips for Agencies & MICE Planners

Last updated: October 2026

A fam trip lets you see the hotels, venues and people behind a destination before you recommend it to a client. DMC Turkey Partner has operated in Turkey since 2006 and can plan and run a fam trip for your agency or client group in Antalya and Belek, with one local team handling the logistics.

Plan a Fam TripSee the 2027 Programme

Who a Fam Trip Is For

  • Incentive and MICE agencies choosing a destination for upcoming client programmes.
  • Corporate event and group travel planners who want to inspect venues first-hand.
  • Overseas agencies that need a local partner to host their own buyers or key clients.

What We Can Arrange

Hotel & venue inspections

Site visits to resorts, meeting hotels and event venues in Antalya and Belek, scheduled around your questions.

Supplier meetings

Introductions to the teams behind transport, production, staffing and on-site coordination.

Transfers & logistics

Airport transfers and ground transport for the whole itinerary.

Evening & incentive experiences

Dinners and group activities that show how a programme would feel for your guests.

Need your agency to keep the client relationship? See white-label DMC support.

Antalya and Belek at a Glance

Antalya offers city hotels, conference venues and a major airport; Belek concentrates large 5-star resorts used for incentive travel and corporate retreats. Read the destination guides for venues, transfers and seasonality: Antalya and Belek. For budgeting, see the published event cost ranges and the event budget calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fam trip?

A familiarisation (fam) trip lets agency and corporate buyers see hotels, venues and suppliers in person before they recommend a destination to clients. A local DMC builds the itinerary and runs the logistics on the ground.

Can you organise a fam trip for our own agency or client group?

Yes. Send us the destination, the number of guests and the dates you have in mind. We reply within 24 hours and send an itemised proposal within 3–5 business days. Scope, inclusions and costs are agreed in writing before anything is booked.

Which destinations can a fam trip cover?

Antalya and Belek are the focus of this page. We also operate in Istanbul, Cappadocia and Bodrum, so a multi-destination itinerary is possible.

Is the 2027 fam trip the same thing?

No. This page describes fam trips we organise for your group. The 2027 Antalya and Belek programme is a separate, invitation-based trip for agencies, described on its own page, and its details remain subject to written confirmation.

Can the fam trip run under our agency’s brand?

Where appropriate, yes. We can work white-label: your agency keeps the relationship with its guests and we handle local execution.
