Overview

Eksim Holding Management Summit was delivered at Calista Luxury Resort in Belek, Antalya, as a multi-service corporate programme for the group's management team.

Scope

Décor and branding, stage production, sound and lighting, overall event production, on-site staffing and guest transportation and logistics.

Execution

Our team managed the full on-ground production chain — from stage and décor build through technical setup, staffing and transportation — for the summit.
