555th Year Interactive Museum

Antalya

Overview

555th Year Interactive Museum (555. Yıl İnteraktif Müzesi) was an experiential exhibition project delivered for Fatih Sultan Mehmet Vakıf University. Unlike a standard conference programme, this project centred on an interactive exhibition format.

Scope

Experiential installation design and build, and overall production of the interactive exhibition space.

Execution

Our team produced the interactive exhibition installations for the anniversary programme.
