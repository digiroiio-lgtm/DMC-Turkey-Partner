Exhibiting at COP31 Antalya: What Every First-Time Exhibitor Needs to Know

COP31 opens at the Antalya EXPO Center on 9 November 2026 and runs for 12 days. If you are exhibiting for the first time, this guide covers the critical decisions: Blue Zone vs Green Zone, stand production timelines, what a local Antalya contractor provides, and what to confirm right now. With 6 weeks to go, some items — accommodation, airport transfers, custom stand fabrication — need to be locked in immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions — COP31 Exhibitors

What is the difference between the Blue Zone and Green Zone at COP31?

The Blue Zone at COP31 is the official UNFCCC-managed area at the Antalya EXPO Center, accessible only to accredited party negotiators, UN observers, and registered organisations. The Green Zone is a parallel public-facing area managed by the host country Turkey, open to NGOs, businesses, and civil society with a lower accreditation barrier. National pavilions and formal country delegations are always in the Blue Zone. Corporate side events and advocacy exhibitions can operate in either zone depending on accreditation status.

How far in advance should I book an exhibition stand for COP31?

For COP31 Antalya, exhibitors should aim to confirm their stand contractor and floor plan by October 2026 at the latest — 4–6 weeks before the event opens on 9 November 2026. At this stage (late September 2026), stand bookings are still possible but lead times for custom fabrication are running short. Shell scheme options are faster to produce than custom builds. Key items to book immediately: stand contractor, accommodation, and private airport transfers. AV, furniture, printing and staffing can follow within 2–3 weeks.

What does a local Antalya exhibition stand contractor provide for COP31?

A local Antalya contractor for COP31 can supply: stand design and fabrication (shell scheme or custom build), branded signage and graphics, AV equipment (screens, sound, lighting), furniture and flooring, coffee machine and hospitality setup, and on-site installation and dismantling crew. DMC Turkey Partner coordinates all these as an integrated package. What the contractor cannot provide: your own promotional materials (printed in advance of shipping), branded merchandise, and official UNFCCC accreditation.

What accommodation is available near the COP31 Antalya EXPO Center?

The Antalya EXPO Center is located in the Döşemealtı district, approximately 25–35 minutes from Antalya city centre and 20–30 minutes from Antalya Airport (AYT). Nearest 5-star hotels include Titanic Mardan Palace (Antalya Beach, ~35 min), Rixos Downtown Antalya (~20 min), and Delphin hotels in Lara Beach (~30 min). During COP31, Antalya hotel availability will be extremely tight across all tiers; block bookings confirmed by October 2026 will be at significantly better rates than spot bookings during the event.

Do I need a local event manager for COP31 or can I manage the stand remotely?

A local on-the-ground event manager is strongly recommended for COP31 first-timers. At large UN conferences, on-site decisions happen in real time: last-minute location changes, accreditation delays, equipment faults, and supplier coordination all require someone physically present with local contacts and Turkish-language capability. DMC Turkey Partner provides dedicated on-site event support for COP31 exhibitors, covering stand management, staff coordination, transfers, and emergency logistics throughout the 12-day conference.
