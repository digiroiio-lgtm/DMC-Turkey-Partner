Ground Handling for Groups, MICE & Events in Turkey

In a DMC context, ground handling means destination and group ground operations for travel, MICE and event programmes. It covers arrival coordination, departure operations, hotel communication, supplier alignment, group movement support and on-site programme delivery. It does not refer to aircraft, cargo or airline ramp handling. The focus is on connecting the local components of an international group programme into a workable operational sequence.

Ground Handling Services We Coordinate

Arrival Operations

Group arrival schedules, flight information, arrival waves, airport-to-hotel coordination and operational communication are planned around the programme start.

Departure Operations

Departure schedules, hotel departures, group sequencing and flight-related timing are coordinated with the wider closing programme.

Airport-to-Hotel Coordination

The interface between flight arrivals, guest groups, vehicles, hotels and programme timing is coordinated with Transportation & Logistics.

Hotel Arrival & Group Coordination

Rooming information, arrival timing, hotel communication and group check-in flow can be aligned with Hotel Sourcing requirements.

Local Supplier Coordination

Transport providers, hotels, venues, production suppliers, activities and restaurants can be aligned around the approved programme schedule.

On-Site Programme Operations

Supplier timing, programme sequencing, group movement, operational communication and local issue resolution are coordinated during delivery.

Ground Handling Is More Than an Airport Transfer

For international groups, ground handling begins with arrivals but continues across the local programme. Hotel readiness, transport schedules, venue timing, supplier arrivals, event setup, daily changes and departure sequencing can all affect how the programme operates. The purpose is to connect these local components so an agency does not have to manage each supplier independently. This is operational coordination for a destination programme, not consumer transfer sales.

  1. Share the Operations Brief

  3. Coordinate Local Suppliers

  4. Build the Operational Schedule

    Arrivals, departures, daily programme, hotel, transport and event timing are brought into one operating sequence.

  5. Local Programme Execution

    The agreed local operations are coordinated through delivery of the programme.

Ground Handling Integrated with Transportation

Ground handling focuses on arrival and departure operations, group coordination, supplier communication and local programme execution. Transportation & Logistics focuses on vehicles, routing, transfer schedules, shuttles and movement operations. The two services work together: one connects the local delivery, while the other structures the transport operation needed for it.

Ground Handling Integrated with Hotels, Venues & Events

Hotel arrival timing affects group flow, venue call times affect programme movement, event setup can change guest arrival timing and supplier schedules can affect the daily plan. We coordinate these dependencies with hotel sourcing, venue sourcing, event production and meetings and conferences requirements to keep the operational plan connected.

Ground Handling Turkey FAQ

What does ground handling mean for a DMC in Turkey?

It means destination and group ground operations: arrivals, departures, hotel coordination, suppliers and on-site programme support. It does not refer to airline or aircraft handling.

Can you coordinate group airport arrivals and departures?

Yes. Flight patterns, group timing, hotel movements and the programme start or end can be coordinated without implying restricted airport access.

Can ground handling be combined with transportation?

Yes. Transportation & Logistics can manage the vehicle, route and schedule requirements alongside local operations.

Can you coordinate hotels and programme suppliers?

Yes. Hotel communication, transport, venues, production and relevant programme suppliers can be aligned around the operational schedule.

Do you support MICE and conference groups?

Yes. See Meetings & Conferences for the wider meeting and delegate planning scope.

Which destinations can you support?

Our current destination pages cover Istanbul, Antalya, Belek, Bodrum and Cappadocia.
