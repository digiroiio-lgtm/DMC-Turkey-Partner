Hotel Sourcing for Groups, Meetings & Events in Turkey

Hotel sourcing for a B2B programme is about operational suitability, not simply room price. We help agencies, MICE planners and tour operators evaluate group accommodation around room blocks, meeting requirements, arrival patterns and the wider programme schedule. The accommodation component is considered in relation to destination fit, local movements, event venues and delegate needs, so it can support the practical delivery of a meeting, incentive, corporate event or multi-day group programme.

What We Consider When Sourcing Your Hotel

Destination & Location

Property location is considered in relation to airports, meeting and event venues, planned activities and transport routes. The objective is a location that works with the programme rather than creating avoidable movement issues.

Room Requirements

We review guest numbers, room categories, single, twin or double occupancy, arrival and departure patterns, and the room-block requirements needed to accommodate the group structure.

Meetings & Event Space

For MICE programmes, the brief can include meeting rooms, breakouts, conference setup, event flow and F&B requirements. These needs help determine whether accommodation and programme activity can work together.

Group Logistics

Coach access, arrival flow, airport transfers, group movements and programme timings all affect accommodation suitability. These details are considered alongside the hotel requirement from the beginning.

Programme Profile

The right hotel approach depends on whether the programme is a conference, incentive, corporate event, group tour or executive meeting. Each format creates a different balance of accommodation and operational needs.

Hotel Sourcing Process

  1. Share Your Brief

    Provide destination, dates, group size, room count and configuration, programme type, meeting requirements and, where available, an approximate budget range.

  2. Property Shortlisting

    Suitable accommodation options are assessed against the programme requirements, destination context and practical logistics for the group.

  3. Proposal & Coordination

    Relevant hotel options and the operational considerations around each are structured for agency or planner review.

  4. Programme Integration

    Once selected, accommodation requirements are coordinated with transfers, venues, meetings, events, activities and ground handling as needed.

More Than Hotel Booking

Hotel sourcing is part of destination management, not an OTA-style room booking service. For groups and MICE programmes, the most suitable hotel is not always simply the lowest room rate. Location, movement times, meeting requirements, coach access and the wider programme schedule can be equally important. We can connect accommodation planning to transportation and logistics, venue sourcing, event production and ground handling, allowing the local delivery to be assessed as one operating plan.

Information We Need to Source the Right Hotels

A clear brief helps us evaluate accommodation against the right operational priorities. Include as much of the following as is available:

  • Destination and arrival/departure dates
  • Number of guests and rooms
  • Single, twin or double requirements
  • Hotel category or positioning
  • Approximate budget range
  • Meeting room and F&B requirements
  • Event and programme requirements
  • Transfer needs and operational constraints

Hotel Sourcing Turkey FAQ

Can you source hotels for large groups in Turkey?

Yes. We assess group accommodation requirements including room count, room configuration, dates, destination and programme logistics, then structure suitable options for the project brief.

Can you source hotels with meeting and conference facilities?

Yes. Meeting rooms, breakout needs, conference setup and F&B requirements can be included in the accommodation brief so hotel and meeting requirements are considered together.

Can hotel sourcing be combined with airport transfers and transportation?

Yes. Accommodation planning can be coordinated with Transportation & Logistics, including airport transfers, routing and group movements.

Which destinations in Turkey can you support?

Our current destination coverage includes Istanbul, Antalya, Belek, Bodrum and Cappadocia. The appropriate accommodation approach depends on the programme format and requirements.

Do you support incentive and corporate event groups?

Yes. Hotel sourcing can be integrated with the activities, dining, venues, production and local logistics needed for incentive programmes and corporate events.

What information do you need before sourcing hotel options?

Destination, dates, group and room numbers, room configuration, hotel positioning, meeting needs, budget guidance and other programme or transfer requirements provide the strongest starting brief.
