FAQ

What does a Turkey DMC handle?

Hotel sourcing and contracting, transfers and coaches, venues, event production, incentive programmes, conferences, ground handling and on-site staffing — the full local execution layer.

What is the difference between a DMC and a travel agency?

A DMC is a B2B local operations partner for agencies, corporations and PCOs. A travel agency sells flights and packages to consumers; DMC Turkey Partner does not sell retail flights.

Which destinations in Turkey does DMC Turkey Partner cover?

Istanbul, Antalya, Belek, Cappadocia and Bodrum, alone or as a multi-city programme. See Destinations.

What group sizes do you work with?

From about 15 guests for incentives and group travel to 500+ for conferences and corporate events; 50–300 delegates is typical.

Can you coordinate hotels, transportation and venues together?

Yes. They run as one local operation from airport arrival to departure; your agency books the international flights.

Can you provide white-label DMC services in Turkey?

Yes. Proposals, supplier communication and on-site coordination can run under your agency’s brand. See White-Label DMC Turkey.

How do we request a proposal?

Send a brief through the Request a Proposal form with destination, group size, dates and services. We reply within 24 hours and send an itemised proposal within 3–5 business days. Once you confirm, a named coordinator is assigned to your programme.
