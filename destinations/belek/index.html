Belek Golf & Resort Destination — MICE, Conference Hotels & Incentive Programmes

DMC Turkey Partner plans and executes MICE programmes, golf incentives, corporate conferences, and group stays in Belek — Turkey’s premier resort corridor on the Antalya coast. Belek is 30–45 minutes from Antalya Airport (AYT) and home to 15+ championship golf courses and Turkey’s highest-capacity resort conference hotels.

What DMC Turkey Partner Handles in Belek

Resort Hotel Sourcing

Matching group profiles with the right Belek resort property. All major hotels with MICE wings (500–1,200 pax) covered, including contract negotiations and rooming list management.

Conferences & Meetings

Coordination of conference room allocation, AV and technical requirements, delegate flow, and agenda-based programme scheduling within resort properties.

Group Transportation

Airport transfers from AYT to Belek (30–45 min). Inter-resort movements. Golf transfer shuttle coordination between hotels and courses.

Golf Incentive Programmes

Golf round bookings across all 15+ Belek courses, tournament organisation, handicap coordination, prize logistics, and post-golf gala dinners. Budget from €600/pax (4 nights, 2 rounds).

Event Production

Gala dinner staging, outdoor function lighting, branding and AV for resort ballrooms and beach or garden venues.

Ground Handling

Arrivals coordination at AYT, hotel check-in management, group movement between resort areas, and on-site operational point throughout the programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which airport serves Belek?

Antalya Airport (AYT) serves Belek, with a 30–45 minute transfer (30–40 km). Direct flights to AYT operate from London, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Moscow, Vienna, Warsaw, Dubai, and other European hubs — AYT is one of the most accessible resort airports in the eastern Mediterranean.

What are the largest conference hotels in Belek?

Rixos Premium Belek (1,200 pax ballroom), Cornelia Diamond Golf Resort (1,000 pax), Regnum Carya (800 pax), Gloria Golf Resort (700 pax), Kempinski The Dome (600 pax), Maxx Royal Belek (500 pax). All include full conference facilities, dedicated AV, F&B, and outdoor event spaces.

Is Belek suitable for golf incentive programmes?

Yes. Belek has 15+ championship courses adjacent to MICE resort hotels. Golf incentive programmes typically combine half-day conference sessions with 18-hole rounds. Best golf season: March–June and October–November. Green fees: approximately €80–150 per round depending on course and season.

What is the best season for MICE events in Belek?

March–June and September–November (18–28°C). Resort hotels operate in full conference mode. July–August is peak leisure season with higher rates and reduced conference wing availability.

What budget should I plan for a Belek conference or incentive?

A 4-night 5-star resort programme (accommodation, all meals, conference room hire, one activity, gala dinner) typically ranges from €600–950 per person. Golf add-ons (green fees, buggy, caddy) add approximately €150–250 per person per round.
