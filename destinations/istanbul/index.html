DMC Istanbul

Plan a MICE or Corporate Event in Istanbul

Istanbul is suited to programmes that combine international access, meeting infrastructure and a city experience. For agencies and corporate planners, a local DMC can coordinate the moving parts between arrival, hotel, venue and programme activity.

What an Istanbul DMC Can Coordinate

  • Hotel sourcing and group room blocks
  • Meeting, conference and dinner venue sourcing
  • Airport transfers, coaches and VIP movements
  • Registration, staffing and on-site coordination
  • Event production and technical supplier coordination
  • City programmes for meetings, incentives and corporate groups

Planning an Istanbul Programme

  1. Share the brief

    Start with dates, expected attendee profile, group size, accommodation needs, meeting requirements and programme objectives.

  2. Build the local scope

    Accommodation, venues, transfers, production and programme elements are assessed together so dependencies are visible early.

  3. Coordinate delivery

    Once the scope is agreed, local suppliers and on-site operations are coordinated through a single operational point.
