Antalya MICE Destination — DMC Services, Resort Hotels & COP31 2026

DMC Turkey Partner is a Turkey-based destination management company operating in Antalya since 2006. We plan and execute MICE programmes, incentive trips, corporate conferences, and group travel for international agencies and PCOs across Antalya city, the Belek resort corridor, and Antalya EXPO Center — the host venue for COP31 in November 2026.

What DMC Turkey Partner Handles in Antalya

Hotel & Resort Sourcing

Antalya city hotels and Belek resort hotels (30–45 min from AYT) including 4-star and 5-star properties with dedicated conference wings up to 1,600 pax.

Venue Sourcing

Antalya EXPO Center (2,500+ pax congress), resort hotel ballrooms (800–1,600 pax), outdoor gala settings, and unique cultural venues across the region.

COP31 Antalya 2026 — Local Event Services

COP31 (the 31st UN Climate Change Conference) takes place 9–20 November 2026 at Antalya EXPO Center. DMC Turkey Partner is providing local event services for organisations exhibiting, presenting, and operating at COP31.

Available COP31 services include: exhibition stand design and build, national pavilion services, AV and equipment rental, interpretation and translation, event staffing, airport transfers from AYT, group accommodation, and last-minute support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which airport serves Antalya for MICE groups?

Antalya Airport (AYT) is the main gateway, 13 km from city centre; transfer is 15–30 minutes. Groups travelling to Belek resort hotels should allow 30–45 minutes from AYT. AYT operates direct flights from London, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Moscow, Dubai, Vienna and Warsaw, making it one of the most accessible resort destinations in the eastern Mediterranean.

What is the largest conference venue capacity in Antalya?

Antalya EXPO Center accommodates 2,500+ delegates in plenary configuration and is the host venue for COP31 in November 2026. For resort-based events, Titanic Mardan Palace handles up to 1,600 pax, Barut Lara up to 1,000 pax, and Rixos Premium Antalya up to 800 pax. In Belek, Rixos Premium Belek seats up to 1,200 pax.

When is the best season for MICE events in Antalya?

The primary MICE season runs March–June and September–November, with temperatures of 18–28°C and resort hotels on full conference-service mode. Peak summer (July–August) is dominated by leisure tourism, which raises hotel rates and reduces MICE-focused service availability. Winter months offer lower rates but fewer outdoor programme options.

What does DMC Turkey Partner provide in Antalya?

We provide hotel and resort sourcing across Antalya city and the Belek corridor, venue sourcing, group airport transfers from AYT, ground handling, event production, incentive programme delivery, and full COP31 local support at Antalya EXPO Center. Group sizes from 30 to 2,500+ pax.

What budget should I plan for an incentive programme in Antalya?

A 4-night incentive programme at a Belek or Lara Beach 5-star resort typically ranges from €550–850 per person, including accommodation, meals, transport, a gala dinner, and two programme activities. Budgets vary depending on hotel category, group size, programme complexity, and season.
