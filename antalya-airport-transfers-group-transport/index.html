Arriving at Antalya Airport

Antalya Airport (AYT) is the arrival point for almost every international group, and sits on the eastern side of the city, close to Lara, Kundu and the Belek resort corridor.

  • Conference and incentive arrivals cluster into one or two days — the airport is busiest exactly when your group needs it.
  • Group arrivals across several flights are far easier with a meet-and-greet point and staged vehicles than with individual bookings.
  • Teams travelling with equipment need vehicles sized for the load, not the headcount.
  • Late-evening and early-morning arrivals are common on charter and Gulf routes; confirm your arrangement covers them.
  • Departure days are similarly concentrated — book return transport at the same time as arrival.

Airport transfer routes below covers arrival and departure movement specifically.

The distinction that matters most is between per-journey and per-day booking. Individual transfers work until the schedule slips; a vehicle on disposal absorbs the slip. For groups with client meetings or press commitments, per-day is almost always the right call — see private transfers and vehicles with driver.

Local and Public Transport

Antalya has a tram and municipal bus network serving the city centre and the coastal areas, and it is genuinely useful for evenings in town. It is not a realistic primary route between resort-corridor hotels and a venue for guests working to a schedule, particularly with luggage, equipment or a fixed appointment. Treat it as an option for personal time rather than as programme transport.

Planning Movement for a Group

  1. Map the fixed points first

    Sessions, client meetings, press moments and hosted dinners. Everything else can flex around them.

  2. Decide the unit of booking

    Per journey for simple patterns; per day for anything that changes. Mixing the two is usually cheapest.

  3. Size vehicles to the real load

    Guests plus luggage plus materials, not headcount alone.

  4. Plan peak days separately

    Gala nights, plenary days and departure day are the hardest periods for vehicles, roads and timing. Over-resource them deliberately.

  5. Name a single coordinator

    One person on your side and one locally, rather than several people booking against each other.

  6. Book return transport with outbound

    Departure day is as concentrated as arrival day.

Meet & Greet and Group Arrivals

For a delegation arriving across several flights, the arrival plan matters more than the vehicles.

  1. Send the flight list

    Flight numbers, arrival times, passenger counts and luggage expectations for each.

  2. We build the arrival plan

    A meeting point, staff on the ground, and vehicles staged rather than all waiting at once.

  3. Meet and greet

    Named greeter at arrivals, luggage assistance, and a holding point for early arrivals waiting on a later flight.

  4. Consolidated or individual departure

    Groups combined where flights are close together, split where they are not.

  5. Live handling of delays

    Flight monitoring so a delayed arrival does not mean a vehicle that has already left.

For movement throughout the conference rather than arrivals alone, see private transfers and vehicles with driver.

Transfer or Disposal?

The single most consequential booking decision, and the one most often got wrong.

Per-journey transfers vs hourly disposal
Per-journey transferHourly / daily disposal
How it worksA vehicle booked for a specific A→B movementA vehicle and driver assigned to you for a period
Best forPredictable, well-spaced movementsDense schedules, client meetings, press, anything that shifts
When the schedule slipsThe booking is wasted; a new vehicle must be foundThe driver waits; the day continues
Cost per journeyLowerHigher
Cost per problemHigh — a missed meeting costs more than a vehicleLow
Typical useArrivals, departures, one-off evening movementsConference days, gala nights, executive programmes

Most delegations end up with a mix: disposal vehicles for principals and dense days, transfers for everything predictable. We will propose a pattern from your schedule rather than sell you the more expensive option by default.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we get from Antalya Airport to our hotel?

Pre-booked private transfers are the most reliable option during the conference peaks, particularly for groups arriving across multiple flights.

Can you provide vehicles for a whole conference or incentive?

Yes — cars, vans, minibuses and coaches with drivers, on transfer or daily-disposal basis. The local driver pool is finite in peak weeks, so early booking matters.

Is public transport practical for a group?

Antalya's tram and bus network is useful for city-centre travel and evenings out, but it is not a practical primary route between resort-corridor hotels and a venue for guests on a schedule.

Do you provide airport transfers in Antalya?

Yes — private and group transfers between Antalya Airport, hotels and venues, with VIP vehicles, minivans, minibuses and coaches.

How far is Antalya Airport from the main hotel areas?

City hotels are 15–30 minutes from the terminal and Belek resorts 30–45 minutes under normal traffic. On busy days, journey time depends more on departure timing and traffic than on distance.

What private transport do you offer in Antalya?

Chauffeured VIP vehicles, vans, minivans, minibuses and coaches, on per-journey transfer or hourly and daily disposal, for group movement, hotel–venue runs, executive dinners and evening events.

What is hourly disposal?

A vehicle and driver assigned to you for a block of hours or a full day. The driver waits between movements, so a schedule change costs nothing rather than requiring a new booking.
