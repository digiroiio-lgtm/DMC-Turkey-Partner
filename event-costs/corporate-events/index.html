How Much Does a Corporate Event in Turkey Cost?

Corporate events in Turkey range from resort-based team programmes and product launches to city-hotel conferences and gala dinners. Cost is driven primarily by destination (resort all-inclusive vs. city itemised pricing), hotel category, group size and production level.
