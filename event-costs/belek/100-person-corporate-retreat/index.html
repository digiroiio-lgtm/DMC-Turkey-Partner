100-Person Corporate Retreat in Belek: Example Cost

A 4-night corporate retreat for 100 employees at a 5-star all-inclusive resort in Belek, combining a half-day strategy session with team-building activities and a closing dinner.

What Could Change This Budget?

Season, hotel category and the scale of the team-building programme (golf, watersports, offsite activities) are the main swing factors. Adding a second activity day or upgrading to a luxury resort increases the budget.

Can This Programme Cost Less?

Shifting to shoulder season, choosing a 4-star all-inclusive resort, and using on-site rather than off-site team-building activities can lower costs.

What Would Make It More Premium?

A golf day, a private beach or villa function, curated wellness programming, and a themed closing dinner with entertainment would move this into a premium tier.
