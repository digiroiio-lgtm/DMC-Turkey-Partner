Event & Conference Costs in Belek

Belek concentrates some of Turkey's largest conference-capable 5-star resorts within a compact, golf-and-spa resort district near Antalya. This makes it a strong fit for conferences, incentive programmes and corporate retreats that want resort-level accommodation and hosted functions without splitting the group across multiple properties.

Indicative budget: €130,000–€175,000 · 4 nights.

What Drives Cost in Belek

Hotel category and season are the largest swing factors, followed by group size, programme level (standard vs. premium production and experiences) and duration. Peak summer (July–August) and Turkish public holidays carry a premium; shoulder season (April–June, September–October) typically offers better rates and availability for larger groups.
