How Much Does an Incentive Trip to Turkey Cost?

On an incentive the experiences line is doing the work: 12-15% of the total in our published 100-person Antalya scenario, against 8-10% for production. That is close to the inverse of a conference, and it is why an incentive costs more per head.

Incentive trips to Turkey are typically built around all-inclusive resorts in Antalya or Belek, with activities, hosted dinners and recognition moments layered on top of accommodation. Group size, hotel category and the richness of the experience programme are the main cost drivers.

What Drives an Incentive Budget

An incentive is the one programme type where the experiences line is doing the actual work. In our published 100-person Antalya incentive, experiences and excursions run 12–15% and AV only 8–10% — close to the inverse of the conference profile, where production dominates and content is incidental.

Indicative share of total programme budget
Cost lineShareNotes
Accommodation (all-inclusive)30–35%The base the whole programme is built on
Experiences & excursions12–15%What the reward actually is. The line to protect
Transfers & transport8–10%Higher than a conference, because the group moves more
F&B beyond the room rate8–10%Hosted dinners and the closing gala
AV / production8–10%Largely the gala; there is no plenary to rig
Staffing & DMC operations8–10%Guides, hosts and on-site coordination
Contingency5%Held, not spent by default

The published 100-person Antalya incentive sits at €140,000–190,000, or roughly €1,400–1,900 per guest, with flights excluded and quoted separately. That is above a comparable conference per head, and it should be — a conference buys a room, an incentive buys a memory.

Budget Questions We Get Asked

Why does an incentive cost more per head than a conference?

Because the value is in the parts a conference does not buy: private venues, closed sites, hosted experiences and a gala that has to feel like an occasion rather than a dinner. Accommodation and transport are comparable; the difference sits almost entirely in the experiences and F&B lines.

What should we protect if the budget tightens?

The experiences. Trimming them to protect the hotel standard produces a nice holiday that nobody remembers as a reward, which defeats the point of the programme. Move dates, drop a night, or reduce the group before you reduce what the group actually does.

How much does the destination change the figure?

Less than the content does, within Türkiye. Antalya and Belek sit close together on accommodation; Bodrum runs higher for smaller premium groups; Cappadocia adds internal travel but delivers experiences that cannot be bought elsewhere. The programme design moves the number more than the map does.

Can we split the group across standards?

Yes, and it is common — a senior tier in suites or a separate property, the main group in the base hotel. It works when the shared moments are genuinely shared and the difference is in the room rather than in the programme. Handled badly it is the fastest way to sour an incentive.

Are flights included?

No. Flights are excluded from every published range and quoted separately, because they depend entirely on where the winners are flying from and that varies far more than anything happening on the ground.
