100-Person Incentive Trip in Antalya: Example Cost

A 4-night reward and recognition trip for 100 participants at a 5-star all-inclusive resort in Antalya, combining leisure time with a hosted excursion, a recognition dinner and a closing gala.

Indicative Programme Budget

€140,000–€190,000

Estimated Cost Per Guest

€1,400–€1,900 per guest

What Could Change This Budget?

Season, hotel category and the number/scale of hosted excursions are the main swing factors. Adding a second excursion day, upgrading to a luxury (rather than 5-star) resort, or including centrally booked flights will materially increase the budget.

Can This Programme Cost Less?

Shifting to shoulder season, reducing to a single hosted excursion, and choosing a 4-star all-inclusive resort can reduce the per-guest cost.

What Would Make It More Premium?

A signature off-site experience (private venue, cultural or adventure activity), a themed gala with live entertainment, premium gifting, and a dedicated on-site incentive host team would elevate this programme.
