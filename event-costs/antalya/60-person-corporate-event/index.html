60-Person Corporate Event in Antalya: Example Cost

A 5-night corporate event for 60 delegates at a 5-star all-inclusive resort in Antalya, combining a half-day business session with hosted group functions and a closing gala dinner.

Indicative Programme Budget

€60,000–€80,000

Estimated Cost Per Guest

€1,000–€1,350 per guest

What Could Change This Budget?

Season is the largest swing factor: peak summer (July–August) and public holidays carry a premium over shoulder season (April–June, September–October). Upgrading hotel category, adding a second gala evening, or increasing AV/production scope will also raise the budget.

Can This Programme Cost Less?

Shifting to shoulder season, choosing a 4-star all-inclusive resort, reducing the programme to a single hosted evening, and using standard rather than premium AV can bring the per-guest cost down.

What Would Make It More Premium?

A private beach or terrace gala, live entertainment or a name performer, a branded welcome experience, premium AV and staging, and curated off-site excursions would move this programme into a premium tier.
