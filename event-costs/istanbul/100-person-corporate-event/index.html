100-Person Corporate Event in Istanbul: Example Cost

A 3-night corporate event for 100 delegates at a 5-star city hotel in Istanbul, with a full-day business session, hosted dinner and an optional city experience.

Indicative Programme Budget

€150,000–€210,000

Estimated Cost Per Guest

€1,500–€2,100 per guest

What Could Change This Budget?

Because Istanbul pricing is itemised rather than all-inclusive, venue choice, F&B menu selection and traffic-driven transfer logistics have a larger relative impact than in a resort destination. City-centre traffic patterns can also affect transfer routing and timing costs.

Can This Programme Cost Less?

Choosing a hotel with in-house meeting space (avoiding a separate venue rental), simplifying the F&B menu, and reducing group movements around the city can lower costs.

What Would Make It More Premium?

A landmark or Bosphorus-view venue, a hosted cultural experience (private museum or heritage-site access), premium catering, and full technical production would elevate this programme.
