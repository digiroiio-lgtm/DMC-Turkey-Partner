How Much Does a Corporate Event in Istanbul Cost?

Istanbul is Turkey's primary city destination for conferences, congresses and corporate events, offering the strongest international flight connectivity and the largest concentration of meeting-capable city hotels. Unlike Antalya's all-inclusive resort model, Istanbul programmes are typically priced as discrete line items — room, meeting space, F&B and production — which gives more control over spend but requires each element to be budgeted individually.

What Drives Cost in Istanbul

Hotel category and season are the largest swing factors, followed by group size, programme level (standard vs. premium production and experiences) and duration. Peak summer (July–August) and Turkish public holidays carry a premium; shoulder season (April–June, September–October) typically offers better rates and availability for larger groups.
