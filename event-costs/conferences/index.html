How Much Does a Conference in Turkey Cost?

Conference budgets are driven by production, not beds. In our published 200-person Antalya scenario the AV and production line is 18-22% of the total, roughly double the same line on an incentive, because a plenary plus breakouts means several rooms rigged and crewed at once.

Conference budgets in Turkey depend heavily on whether the venue is a resort meeting space (Antalya/Belek, often bundled with accommodation) or a dedicated city/congress venue (Istanbul, priced separately from hotel rooms). Delegate count, session format and AV/production requirements are the other major cost drivers.

What Drives a Conference Budget

Conferences are the one programme type where production, not accommodation, is the swing factor. In our published 200-person Antalya conference the AV and production line runs 18–22% of the total — roughly double what the same line costs on an incentive of comparable size — because a plenary plus breakouts means multiple rooms rigged, staffed and running simultaneously.

Read against our published scenario, a 200-person conference in Antalya sits at €220,000–300,000, or roughly €1,100–1,500 per delegate. Flights are excluded from that and quoted separately.

Budget Questions We Get Asked

What is the biggest lever on a conference budget?

The number of concurrent rooms. Every breakout stream running at the same time needs its own screen, audio, technician and often its own recording, so the AV line scales with the agenda's shape rather than with the delegate count. Compressing a three-stream agenda into two streams saves more than trimming the guest list.

Is a resort or a city venue cheaper for a conference?

A resort usually, because the meeting space comes with the room block instead of being bought separately — you can see it in the numbers, where venue is 5–8% in our Antalya scenario against 8–10% in the Istanbul one, and Istanbul also itemises F&B that the all-inclusive rate absorbs. The city wins when the delegates need the airlift or the agenda needs the city.

Does the cost per delegate fall as the conference grows?

Yes, up to a point. Production and staffing are largely fixed once the room set is decided, so spreading them over more delegates lowers the per-head figure. The saving stops when the group outgrows a single property and you start paying for a second hotel and shuttles between them.

How far out do these numbers hold?

The shape holds; the absolute figures move with the room rate, which is the largest line and the most seasonal. A conference booked into high season in a resort destination will sit at the top of the published range or above it. Spring and autumn sit in the middle.

What is not in the range?

Flights, which we quote separately because they depend entirely on where the delegates start, and anything the client contracts directly — a speaker fee, an agency's own creative, a global AV partner brought in from outside Türkiye.
