How Much Does a Conference in Turkey Cost?

Conference budgets in Turkey depend heavily on whether the venue is a resort meeting space (Antalya/Belek, often bundled with accommodation) or a dedicated city/congress venue (Istanbul, priced separately from hotel rooms). Delegate count, session format and AV/production requirements are the other major cost drivers.
