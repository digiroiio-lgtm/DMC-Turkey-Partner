Antalya & Belek Fam Trip 2027: Programme and Eligibility

Last updated: October 2026

DMC Turkey Partner is planning a familiarisation trip to Antalya and Belek for agencies in 2027. This page outlines the proposed programme, who should apply and how applications are reviewed. Everything below is proposed and subject to written confirmation.

Register Your Interest

Proposed Programme at a Glance

Proposed period: January–March 2027. Exact dates are not confirmed.

  • Length: three nights in Antalya and Belek.
  • Focus: hotel and venue inspections, meeting our local operations team and networking.
  • Format: a small group of agency decision-makers; applications are reviewed individually.

Indicative Itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrival, hotel check-in and welcome dinner.
  2. Day 2Hotel and venue inspections across Antalya and Belek.
  3. Day 3Incentive experience, operations presentation and networking dinner.
  4. Day 4Breakfast, airport transfer and departure.

Indicative programme, subject to confirmation.

What Is Proposed to Be Hosted

  • Accommodation
  • Local transfers
  • Scheduled programme activities

International flights are expected to be arranged by participants. All inclusions, exclusions, dates, eligibility and cancellation conditions remain subject to written confirmation.

Eligibility

The invitation is aimed at incentive and MICE agencies, corporate event and group travel planners, and agency owners, directors and senior decision-makers who manage international group programmes. Priority goes to agencies that organise at least two international group programmes a year.

How to Apply

  1. Register Your Interest

    Complete the short application on the invitation page.

  2. Individual Review

    Our team reviews every application.

  3. Shortlisted Applicants

    We contact shortlisted applicants with dates, inclusions and conditions in writing.

Go to the Application Page

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the 2027 fam trip for?

Incentive and MICE agencies, corporate event and group travel planners, and agency owners, directors and senior decision-makers who manage international group programmes. Priority goes to agencies that organise at least two international group programmes a year.

Are flights included?

No. International flights are expected to be arranged by participants. The proposed hosted elements are accommodation, local transfers and scheduled programme activities.

When does it take place?

The proposed period is January–March 2027. Exact dates have not been confirmed; they will be shared with shortlisted applicants.

How are applications reviewed?

Individually. Our team reviews each application and contacts shortlisted applicants with further information. Registering your interest does not guarantee a place.

Is there a 2026 programme?

No 2026 fam trip programme is published by DMC Turkey Partner. If you need a fam trip sooner, we can plan one for your own group; see our Antalya fam trips service page.
