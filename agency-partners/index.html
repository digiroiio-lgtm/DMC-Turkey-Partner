Agency Partnership FAQ

Who can become an agency partner with DMC Turkey Partner?

International MICE agencies, incentive houses, event agencies, tour operators, group travel specialists, corporate travel companies and PCOs. The partnership model is built for B2B cooperation: the agency retains the client commercial relationship while DMC Turkey Partner handles local ground execution in Turkey.

What does the agency handle vs what does the DMC handle?

The agency owns the client relationship, prepares the client proposal and contracts with the end client. DMC Turkey Partner sources hotels, negotiates rates, contracts and operates ground transport, sources and contracts venues, delivers on-site event production, coordinates local suppliers and manages all logistics — reporting to the agency, not the end client.

Can we use DMC Turkey Partner for a single project?

Yes. A project-based partnership means one brief, defined scope, local sourcing and execution — with no commitment to recurring work. This is the typical entry point for agencies testing a new destination partner.

Can DMC Turkey Partner become our ongoing Turkey execution partner?

Yes. Agencies with recurring Turkey enquiries can set up an ongoing cooperation arrangement: repeat projects, multiple destinations, centralized local execution under a consistent operational relationship.

Can our agency retain the client relationship and brand?

Yes. DMC Turkey Partner supports operational delivery in Turkey while the agency remains client-facing. Under a white-label arrangement, all documents, communications and on-site coordination appear under the agency's identity — the end client does not interact directly with the DMC. See White-Label DMC Turkey.

Which destinations does DMC Turkey Partner cover for agency programmes?

Istanbul (urban conferences and corporate events, year-round), Antalya and Belek (resort MICE and incentive programmes, peak April–October), Bodrum (executive incentives, May–October) and Cappadocia (experience-led incentives, April–November). Multi-destination programmes are coordinated as a single scope. See Destinations.

Can DMC Turkey Partner coordinate hotels, transport, venues and production together?

Yes. For a typical 100-person incentive programme, DMC Turkey Partner can simultaneously source and contract the hotel block, ground transport (airport transfers, coaches, excursion vehicles), dinner and activity venues, and technical production (AV, staging, lighting) — all under one operational plan submitted to the agency.

How do we start working with DMC Turkey Partner?

Send a project brief with: programme dates, group size, destination, accommodation category, transport needs, any venue or production requirements and budget guidance. For agency onboarding without a live project, share your agency type, markets, typical group size and Turkey destinations of interest. Request a Proposal.
