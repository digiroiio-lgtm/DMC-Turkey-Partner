Agency Partnership FAQ

Who can become an agency partner?

International travel agencies, MICE agencies, event agencies, incentive houses, tour operators, group travel specialists and corporate travel companies.

Do you work with travel and MICE agencies outside Turkey?

Yes, this partnership model is built for international agencies looking for local execution in Turkey.

Can we use you for a single project?

Yes, through the project-based partnership model.

Can you become our ongoing DMC partner in Turkey?

Yes, through the ongoing Turkey execution partner model for agencies with recurring requirements.

Can our agency retain the client relationship?

Yes. Your agency remains the primary commercial relationship, and communication can be structured around an agency-led workflow.

Do you provide white-label DMC support?

Which destinations do you cover?

Istanbul, Antalya, Belek, Cappadocia and Bodrum. See Destinations.

Can you coordinate hotels, transport and venues?

Yes, these are coordinated as part of the local operational structure.

Yes. See MICE Turkey for meetings, conferences, corporate events and technical production.
