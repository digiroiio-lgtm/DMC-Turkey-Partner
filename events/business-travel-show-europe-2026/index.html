Event Overview

Business Travel Show Europe is a trade exhibition and conference for the corporate and business travel industry, held at ExCeL London alongside The Meetings Show. It brings together travel management companies, corporate travel buyers, technology providers and suppliers focused on managed business travel programmes.

The event's conference programme typically covers travel policy, duty of care, sustainability and technology, while the exhibition floor focuses on TMCs, booking technology, payment and expense platforms, and travel risk management providers.

Who Attends

  • Corporate travel managers and travel management companies (TMCs)
  • Procurement and finance teams managing travel budgets
  • Business travel technology and payments suppliers
  • MICE and event buyers with a corporate travel remit

Why This Event Matters

As one of Europe's principal events dedicated specifically to managed business travel, it is a relevant reference point for MICE and DMC partners whose work increasingly intersects with corporate travel policy, duty of care and procurement requirements.

What MICE Professionals Should Know

  • Registration is typically free for qualifying corporate travel buyers and travel managers, with paid options for suppliers.
  • The co-located Meetings Show badge often permits access to both events, useful for buyers covering both meetings and travel remits.
  • Sessions frequently address travel risk management and duty of care, topics that are also directly relevant to group and incentive travel programmes.

Planning an Event in Turkey?

Corporate travel buyers assessing duty-of-care and logistics considerations for group travel or incentive programmes in Turkey can find this event a useful complement to MICE-focused shows.
