A specialist education day is scheduled for Monday, 10 May 2027, ahead of the main exhibition days.

Event Overview

IMEX Frankfurt 2027 is the next confirmed edition of the IMEX Group's flagship European trade show for the meetings, incentives, conferences and events (MICE) industry, returning to Messe Frankfurt. As with previous editions, the show is expected to combine a hosted buyer exhibition with an education programme for planners and association executives.

Detailed exhibitor lists, education content and hosted buyer criteria for the 2027 edition are typically published progressively in the months leading up to the show. This page will be updated as further official details are confirmed.

Who Attends

  • Meeting and event planners from corporations, associations and agencies
  • Incentive travel buyers and houses
  • Convention bureaux, hotels, venues and destination management companies
  • Technology suppliers serving the meetings and events industry

Why This Event Matters

As the direct successor to IMEX Frankfurt 2026, the 2027 edition continues the show's role as an annual planning and relationship-building milestone for European MICE buyers, agencies and destinations, including those promoting Turkey as a meetings and incentive destination.

What MICE Professionals Should Know

  • Registration for hosted buyers and exhibitors for the 2027 edition typically opens progressively following the close of the prior year's show.
  • As with earlier editions, most substantive meetings are pre-scheduled rather than arranged on-site.
  • Check the official IMEX Frankfurt website for confirmed exhibitor lists and education programme details as the date approaches.

Planning an Event in Turkey?

Agencies planning European MICE or incentive programmes for 2027, including projects in Turkey, often use the Frankfurt show cycle as a checkpoint for confirming supplier and destination partnerships.
