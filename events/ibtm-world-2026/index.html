Event Overview

IBTM World is a long-running global exhibition for the meetings, incentives, conferences and events industry, held annually in Barcelona and organised by RX Global. Like IMEX, it runs a hosted buyer programme that pairs qualified buyers with exhibitors through a pre-scheduled appointment diary, alongside a general exhibition floor and conference sessions.

The show has historically drawn a strongly international exhibitor base spanning destinations, convention bureaux, hotel groups, DMCs and venues, making it a useful single-location opportunity for agencies to review supplier options across multiple regions.

Who Attends

  • MICE and incentive travel agencies
  • Corporate meeting and event planners
  • Association and congress organisers
  • Destinations, venues, hotels and DMCs

Why This Event Matters

IBTM World's Barcelona base and broad international exhibitor mix make it a key European counterpart to IMEX Frankfurt, and it is frequently used by agencies to benchmark destination and DMC options across Europe, the Middle East and beyond in the same trip.

What MICE Professionals Should Know

  • Hosted buyer applications are reviewed against IBTM's qualification criteria before appointment diaries are issued.
  • The exhibition typically runs alongside an education programme covering association meetings, sustainability and industry trends.
  • Fira Barcelona Gran Via is a large, purpose-built exhibition venue with good connectivity to Barcelona–El Prat Airport.

Planning an Event in Turkey?

Turkey-focused destination management companies are regularly present at IBTM World, reflecting continued demand from European and international agencies for MICE and incentive programmes in Turkey.
