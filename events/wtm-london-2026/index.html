Event Overview

World Travel Market (WTM) London is one of the world's leading global travel trade shows, held annually at ExCeL London. It brings together destinations, tour operators, hotel groups, airlines and technology providers from across the world for three trade-only days.

While its core focus is global travel trade, WTM London carries dedicated content and exhibitor presence relevant to MICE, business travel and responsible tourism, making it relevant to agencies whose remit spans leisure, group and corporate travel.

Who Attends

  • Global travel and tourism trade professionals
  • Destination marketing organisations and tourism boards
  • MICE, incentive and business travel buyers attending trade days
  • Travel technology and distribution suppliers

Why This Event Matters

WTM London's global exhibitor base and London location make it a convenient single-trip opportunity for UK and international agencies to review destination and DMC options across multiple regions, including Turkey, alongside broader travel industry trends.

What MICE Professionals Should Know

  • WTM London is a trade-only event; accreditation as an industry professional is required to attend.
  • The show is organised by region and theme, so meetings can typically be planned around relevant destination halls in advance.
  • ExCeL London is directly served by the Elizabeth line and DLR from central London and both major airports.

Planning an Event in Turkey?

Turkey is typically represented at WTM London through national, regional and private-sector exhibitors, making it a relevant meeting point for UK and international agencies evaluating Turkey for MICE, incentive or group travel programmes.
