MICE & Business Events in Germany

Germany hosts two of the global meetings industry's most significant annual trade shows, in Frankfurt and Berlin.

Germany is one of the most important host countries on the international MICE and travel trade calendar, anchored by IMEX Frankfurt for the meetings and incentive travel industry and ITB Berlin for the broader global travel trade.

Agencies and DMCs active in the German market, or using Germany as a hub for pan-European supplier meetings, will find both cities' events tracked here.
