Money20/20 Europe 2026

Amsterdam, Netherlands · 2 June 2026 – 4 June 2026

Dates
2 June 2026 – 4 June 2026
Venue

City / Country
Amsterdam, Netherlands
Category
Major International Conferences
Status
Concluded

Event Overview

Money20/20 Europe is a major annual conference for the fintech, payments and banking industry, held at RAI Amsterdam. While it is a vertical industry conference rather than a MICE trade show, its scale and international attendance make it a significant driver of corporate hospitality, sponsorship and delegate travel demand each year.

For agencies and DMCs, events of this type are relevant not as MICE trade shows to exhibit at, but as high-value client conferences around which corporate hospitality, executive travel and side-event programmes are frequently built.

Who Attends

  • Fintech, payments and banking executives
  • Corporate event and conference planners organising client attendance
  • Sponsorship, hospitality and delegate management teams
  • Technology and financial services suppliers

Why This Event Matters

Large vertical industry conferences such as Money20/20 Europe generate recurring corporate travel, hospitality and event execution demand for agencies serving financial services and technology clients, distinct from the meetings-industry trade shows covered elsewhere in this calendar.

What MICE Professionals Should Know

  • Attendance is primarily by delegates and sponsors from the fintech, payments and banking sectors rather than the meetings industry itself.
  • Sponsorship and exhibition packages are managed directly by the organiser and are separate from any hosted buyer programme.
  • RAI Amsterdam is a large, centrally located convention centre with strong access to Amsterdam's hotel districts and Schiphol Airport.

Agencies coordinating corporate hospitality or executive travel around large financial services conferences may draw on Turkey-based DMC support for related side events or incentive travel extensions.
