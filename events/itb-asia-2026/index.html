Event Overview

ITB Asia is described by its organisers as Asia's leading B2B travel trade show, held annually in Singapore at the Sands Expo & Convention Centre. It brings together destinations, hotel groups, airlines, technology providers and travel intermediaries serving the Asia-Pacific outbound and inbound markets.

The show includes a dedicated MICE segment alongside its broader leisure travel trade programme, reflecting Singapore's role as a regional business events and travel technology hub.

Who Attends

  • Asia-Pacific travel trade professionals
  • MICE and business travel buyers active in Asia-Pacific
  • Destination marketing organisations and tourism boards
  • Travel technology and distribution suppliers

Why This Event Matters

For destinations and DMCs seeking to grow demand from Asia-Pacific agencies, ITB Asia is a relevant complement to AIME, offering a Singapore-based meeting point with a strong regional travel trade and MICE audience.

What MICE Professionals Should Know

  • ITB Asia is a trade-only event requiring professional accreditation.
  • The show includes dedicated MICE-focused sessions and exhibitor areas alongside general travel trade content.
  • Marina Bay Sands is centrally located in Singapore with direct access to Changi Airport.

Planning an Event in Turkey?

Long-haul destinations, including Turkey, use ITB Asia to build awareness among Asia-Pacific agencies sourcing MICE, incentive and leisure group programmes outside the region.
