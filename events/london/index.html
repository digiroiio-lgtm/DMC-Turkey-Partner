MICE & Business Travel Events in London, United Kingdom

London hosts more of our tracked MICE and business travel events at ExCeL London than any other single city.

London, and specifically ExCeL London, is host to a dense cluster of UK and international meetings, event and business travel trade shows across the year, including two co-located shows held on the same dates.

For agencies covering the UK market, this makes London a particularly efficient base for supplier meetings across the meetings, corporate travel and global travel trade sectors.
