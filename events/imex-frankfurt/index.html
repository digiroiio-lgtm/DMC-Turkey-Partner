IMEX Frankfurt

Frankfurt, Germany · Evergreen event guide · MICE Trade Shows · Incentive Travel

Why IMEX Frankfurt Matters for Turkey DMC

IMEX Frankfurt is the single most important annual trade show for Turkey's destination management industry. It is where European and international agency partners, incentive houses and corporate buyers who commission Turkey programmes — or are evaluating Turkey for the first time — attend. DMC Turkey Partner attends IMEX Frankfurt every year specifically to meet these buyers.

Turkey is one of the most actively promoted MICE destinations at IMEX Frankfurt, represented by Antalya convention bureaux, Belek resort hotels, Istanbul congress infrastructure, and DMC companies. For agencies, the show is the most time-efficient environment in which to meet multiple Turkey destination stakeholders in a single visit.

What We Do at IMEX Frankfurt

At IMEX Frankfurt, DMC Turkey Partner is available for pre-scheduled appointments with agencies and buyers planning Turkey programmes. Our typical meeting agenda covers:

  • Destination briefing for agencies new to Turkey
  • Programme scoping for specific group sizes, destinations and budgets
  • Hotel and venue options for confirmed enquiries
  • White-label DMC arrangement discussions for agencies with existing Turkey clients
  • COP31 Antalya local services briefing (applicable until November 2026)

Appointments are 20–30 minutes and are held on the exhibition floor or in a private meeting room depending on availability. Request a meeting →
