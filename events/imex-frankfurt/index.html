IMEX Frankfurt

Frankfurt, Germany · Evergreen event guide · MICE Trade Shows · Incentive Travel

IMEX Frankfurt is the IMEX Group's flagship European trade show for the meetings, incentives, conferences and events (MICE) industry, held annually at Messe Frankfurt. This page tracks the confirmed editions of the show and links to a dedicated, fact-checked page for each edition.
