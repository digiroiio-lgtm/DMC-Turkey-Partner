LED Tunnels & Entrance Arches

Walk-through LED tunnels, illuminated arches and light corridors that set the tone before guests reach the room.

Stages, LED Walls & Show Lighting

Main stages built around LED walls, truss and moving-light rigs, for plenaries, award nights, open-air ceremonies and live performance.

Conference & Plenary Set-ups

Classroom and theatre-style plenary rooms for dealer meetings, management summits and congresses, with stage, screens and in-room branding.

Registration & Welcome Desks

Branded registration counters, LED-fronted welcome desks and accreditation lines for smooth, on-brand arrivals.

Branded Entrances & Building Wraps
