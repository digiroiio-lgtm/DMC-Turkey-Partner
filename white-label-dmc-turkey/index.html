What Is a White-Label DMC Partnership?

The international agency owns and manages the client relationship. DmcTurkeyPartner.com acts as the local operational partner in Turkey, coordinating local services, suppliers and execution on the ground. The overseas agency remains the primary commercial relationship, and white-label support can be provided where appropriate.

Built for Agencies That Need Local Execution in Turkey

These companies may have their own clients, their own branding, their own account management and their own commercial relationships, but require reliable local execution in Turkey:

  • Travel Agencies
  • MICE Agencies
  • Event Agencies
  • Incentive Houses
  • Tour Operators
  • Group Travel Specialists
  • Corporate Travel Companies
  • Destination Specialists

Your Client Relationship Stays With You

The overseas agency remains the primary relationship with its client. DmcTurkeyPartner.com supports local operational delivery in Turkey, and communication can be structured to protect agency-client continuity. We work within an agency-led communication structure where appropriate, coordinating local operational requirements without unnecessarily disrupting the agency-client relationship.

One Local Team. Multiple Operational Disciplines.

Rather than coordinating many disconnected local suppliers directly, an international agency can centralize local coordination across hotels, transportation, venues, production, staff, activities, suppliers, logistics and on-site operations through a single local structure.

How the Partnership Works

  1. You Send the Brief

    Destination, dates, group size, project type, required services and client expectations.

  2. We Source Locally

    Hotels, transportation, venues, suppliers, production and operational requirements are evaluated.

  3. We Build the Operational Proposal

    The local scope is consolidated into a structured proposal.

  4. Your Agency Manages the Client Relationship

    Your agency continues to manage its commercial relationship and client communication.

  5. We Execute in Turkey

    Local suppliers, logistics, production, staffing and on-site operations are coordinated locally.
